## 📚 My Projects

### 🎨 Task 1: Digital Awareness Campaign Poster :

**Project Overview**

For my first task at SkillCraft Technology, I created a **digital awareness poster** centered around the powerful message: **"Pause. Think. Post."**

The core idea is simple yet impactful: *every post, comment, or share leaves a digital footprint that lasts far longer than a moment.*

#### 🎯 Campaign Message

Before sharing anything online, ask yourself three critical questions:

✅ **Is it TRUE?** - Make sure it's real and not just forwarded.  
✅ **Is it KIND?** - Your words can build up or break down.  
✅ **Is it SAFE?** - Think about your privacy and others' too.  

#### 🎨 Design Process

**Visual Hierarchy & Elements:**
- **Bold Typography**: Large, impactful text with "POST" highlighted in coral red
- **Dark Digital Theme**: Navy and black background representing the digital space
- **Neon Accents**: Glowing blue and coral colors for modern tech feel
- **Central Smartphone**: Illustrates social media interaction and digital presence
- **Icons**: Glowing symbols (heart, lock, chat bubbles) for quick visual recognition
- **Decorative Elements**: Dotted patterns and line dividers for visual interest

**Design Goals:**
- ✨ Create clear visual hierarchy for easy message comprehension
- ✨ Use contrast and bold elements to grab attention
- ✨ Combine creativity with meaningful digital awareness messaging
- ✨ Make it relatable to modern users through smartphone imagery

#### 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Canva** - Main design platform
- **Freepik** - Icons and design elements

#### 💡 Key Takeaways

This project gave me the opportunity to:
- Combine **creativity with social responsibility**
- Create a **visually compelling design** that communicates a serious message
- Develop skills in **typography, color theory, and visual hierarchy**
- Understand how **design can drive awareness and behavior change**
